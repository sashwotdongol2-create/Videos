# Build lessons (binding): every finding and fix from building Shorts, applied from the next Short on

Add a dated entry every time a build finds a problem and fixes it. Each entry says **what went wrong**, **the fix**,
and **the rule** for next time. Read the whole file before planning sets, cast or shots. Related files:
- [DESIGN_DETAIL.md](DESIGN_DETAIL.md): how it should look
- [MOTION_EXPRESSION.md](MOTION_EXPRESSION.md): how it should move
- [../../master-script/references/POSTED_LESSONS.md](../../master-script/references/POSTED_LESSONS.md): why Shorts lose views (when master-script is installed)

Merged into the main skill 2026-11-19. The user's dated rules (RULES.md, WORLD.md, COMPOSITION.md, LOOK -> Acting)
win over a row here: faces stay calm and held (#5 and #42 are read with LOOK -> Acting), and assets near the cast are
built in Blender (#13 and #21 apply to the far background only).

## 2026-11-18: master-kit draft v1 (flat 2D preview), compared with RicksClips frames

What went wrong in the 2D draft, and the rule for the full build:

| # | Finding vs RicksClips | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Characters were tiny and full-body, with empty floor | Medium-close by default; the head sits in the top third; full body only for walks and wides |
| 2 | No set, just a gradient | Never test or ship on an empty background: always a dressed 3D set |
| 3 | Flat colour: no shadow, no depth of field | Key light, soft shadow and depth of field on every shot |
| 4 | Head and body leaned as one paper card | The head moves on the neck (tilt, nod, turn) separately and more than the body |
| 5 | Eyes changed only when the word changed | An eye track faster than the words: glances, widen and squint every 4-6 frames |
| 6 | The mouth flapped on a sine wave | Mouth shapes come from the narration audio, held 2-3 frames |
| 7 | Symbols stayed on for whole lines | A symbol pops in on the key word, holds 10-20 frames, then leaves; at most 1 per shot |
| 8 | The face-colour swap was used on an ordinary line | At most one colour swap per Short, on the emotional peak |
| 9 | One static listener | Background extras with their own small, out-of-sync idle actions |
| 10 | 2D zoom without parallax | Real 3D camera moves (dolly plus a small arc) |
| 11 | Captions too big and bouncy | Captions are small, white with a thin outline, and calm, with no pop |
